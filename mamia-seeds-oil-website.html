Brief History

Mamia Seeds Oil began with a simple idea: Nigeria grows enough soybeans to feed its own kitchens, so why import the oil?

What started as a modest processing operation in Kaduna has grown into one of the region's larger soya oil producers - built on direct sourcing from local farmers, careful pressing, and a refusal to cut corners on quality.

Today, the company runs a full production facility employing hundreds of workers, turning raw soybeans into the bottles and jerry cans that reach kitchens, restaurants, and markets across Nigeria every day.

Bean to Bottle

From soybean to shelf, every step matters.

Five stages stand between a raw soybean and a bottle of Mamia oil - each one done to keep the oil clean and consistent.

01

Sourcing

Soybeans bought directly from local Kaduna-area farmers and inspected for quality.

02

Cleaning

Beans are cleaned and sorted to remove debris before processing begins.

03

Pressing

Beans are pressed to extract raw oil using the factory's processing lines.

04

Refining

Oil is filtered and refined to remove impurities, leaving a clean finish.

05

Bottling

Finished oil is bottled, sealed, and prepared for distribution nationwide.
